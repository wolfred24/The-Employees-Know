# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- First public release as **The Employees Know**.
- Employees can use enabled storages on their property to supply their assigned stations.
- Botanists can use enabled storages for soil, seeds, additives, spawn, drying racks and spawn stations.
- Chemists can fetch ingredients for chemistry stations, mixers, cauldrons and lab ovens from enabled storages.
- Handlers can fetch inputs for packaging stations and brick presses from enabled storages.
- Employees can take items from delivery vans while they are parked at the property.
- Per-storage employee access toggle in the storage menu.
- Removed artificial per-trip and ingredient stock limits so movement is limited only by the game's slot and stack capacity.
- Removed the experimental packaging-slot coordinator. Packaging stations use vanilla behaviour.
- Host-only IL2CPP build.
